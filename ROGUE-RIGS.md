The private delivery includes 38 clip GIFs and numbered sheets, approved-concept
comparisons, day/night gameplay-distance renders, a standing Momentum strip, a
combined Momentum/Cornered view and a three-rank counter strip. Download/open the
self-contained HTML locally; GitHub source view does not execute previews.

Blockbench and browser checks, authoring invariants, local staging/preflight and
all nine local pack gate commands passed. Asset evidence stays private. Actual
BetterModel hooks and in-game visuals are not certified by this draft.

Owner decisions remain recorded in the contracts: proposed L45 knife-count tracking,
cloud footprint discrepancy, zero-stack counter treatment, Cornered's held-state
semantics and velocity selection. Ordinary bursts remain particles. Sidestep is
cast-animation work. Bleed depends on Bloodweaver's future wound-mark family at
Rogue cut intensity. No bleed rig or Pocket Sand burst rig was built.

Both PRs remain drafts. Nothing merged or deployed. Owner visual approval and the
normal executable-tooling review are required before a later merge/publication.
